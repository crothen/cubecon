Privacy Notice

How CubeCon 2026 handles your data. Last updated: 13 April 2026.

Who we are

This website is operated by the CubeCon 2026 organizing team (Aarebogemagic, Switzerland). For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@aarebogemagic.ch.

What data we collect

We only collect data that is strictly necessary to run the cube voting:

  • Voter name: the name you enter when registering via your invite link.
  • Invite code: the unique code contained in your invite link.
  • Your votes: the cubes you select and the order you rank them in.
  • Technical data: standard server logs from our hosting provider (IP address, timestamp, user agent) for security and abuse prevention.

We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party social media trackers on this site.

Why we process it

  • To let you cast and review your votes, and to prevent duplicate voting.
  • To publish anonymized or aggregated voting results at the event.
  • To secure the site against abuse.

Legal basis under GDPR: performance of the voting service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b)) and our legitimate interest in running a fair contest (Art. 6(1)(f)). Under the revised Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP), processing is based on your consent and the necessity of the processing for the service.

Storage and data processors

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Firebase Hosting, and Firebase Authentication), operated by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. Data may be transferred to and processed in data centers outside Switzerland or the EU. Google is certified under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and provides Standard Contractual Clauses for international transfers.

The site uses browser local storage only for keeping you signed in to your active voting session. No tracking cookies are set.

Retention

Voting data is retained until the end of the CubeCon 2026 event and then deleted or anonymized within a reasonable period (typically within three months after the event). Server logs are retained according to Google's standard retention policy.

Your rights

Under GDPR and the Swiss FADP you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to or restrict processing.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (e.g. the Swiss FDPIC or your local EU data protection authority).

To exercise any of these rights, email info@aarebogemagic.ch with your invite code or the name you registered under.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice if the site changes. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.
