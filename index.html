Welcome to CubeCon 2026

Join us for an epic day of cube drafting at Kulturwerk 118, Sursee. Up to 64 players will be divided into 8 pods based on your cube preferences.

This isn't just another tournament—it's a celebration of the cube format. Each pod drafts a different cube, curated by passionate cube owners from across Switzerland.

Tournament Structure & Prizes

Round 1: Cube Draft
Everyone plays one of 8 cubes. The 8 pod winners advance to the finals.

Finals: Vintage Cube
The 8 winners compete in Vintage Cube for the big prizes:

  • 1st — Lion's Eye Diamond
  • 2nd — Survival of the Fittest
  • 3rd / 4th — Force of Will
  • 5th – 8th — Windswept Heath

Consolation Round
Other players get to draft a second cube. Pod winners receive one of:

  • Goblin Welder
  • City of Brass
  • Worldly Tutor
  • Entomb
  • Pernicious Deed
  • Birds of Paradise
  • Wrath of God

If there are significantly fewer than 64 players, the prize pool will be adapted. The structure remains the same.
